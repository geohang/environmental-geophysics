Model assumptionsWhat this simulation simplifies
  • This is a schematic drawing: the field lines, eddy-current loops, and secondary-field paths are fixed shapes, and no field or current is computed.
  • Target Conductivity (σ) has no units: the slider (1 to 100) scales the eddy-current and secondary-field line width (1 to 6 px) and opacity linearly, and its Low, Medium, and High examples are labels only.
  • Only the target carries eddy currents; the host ground carries none and does not weaken the primary field.
  • Frequency, coil spacing, and target depth are fixed, and the secondary field is drawn only along its path to the RX coil.
