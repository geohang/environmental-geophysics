Material Properties

Controls conduction current. For GPR it is the main cause of signal attenuation.

Controls how easily bound molecules stretch (GPR).

What this means for GPR (f = 100 MHz)

Compare the conduction current J = σE with the displacement current ∂D/∂t, whose amplitude is ωεE. Their ratio is the loss tangent.

Loss tangent tanδ = σ/(2πfε0εr)0.072
Attenuation α ≈ 1636σ/√εr (σ in S/m)3.27 dB/m
Velocity v = 0.3/√εr0.060 m/ns

tanδ < 1: wave propagation dominates (GPR works).
