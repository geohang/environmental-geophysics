Model assumptionsWhat this simulation simplifies
  • The diagram is one plane in which the Earth field points up the screen (north). Induced magnetization Ji = k × F lies along it, and the Remanence Angle is measured clockwise from it.
  • Magnetizations are given in nT (the field each is equivalent to). Jtot is the vector sum Ji + Jr, and Q = Jr/Ji compares magnitudes only, whatever the angle.
  • Susceptibility is one isotropic constant with no self-demagnetization, which starts to matter near the top of the k range (about 0.1 SI and above).
  • The app stops at the rock's magnetization: there is no body shape, depth, or anomaly profile.
  • Rock presets are single illustrative values of k, Jr, and angle, with F reset to 50,000 nT; real rock types span wide ranges.
