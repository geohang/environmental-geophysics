Model assumptionsWhat this simulation simplifies
  • The animations are qualitative, in relative units: no electric potential is computed, and the voltage bar only labels the polarity; its length does not change with the slider.
  • Streaming: grains are 2-D circles with a fixed negative surface charge. Bulk positive and negative ions move with the water at the same speed, so only the extra positive ions of the diffuse layer carry net charge downstream, at a rate that grows with Fluid Flow Speed.
  • The return conduction current is represented by collected ions jumping back to the upstream side of a grain: each has a fixed chance per frame, so the return grows with the collected charge.
  • Redox: the ore body is a tilted rectangle, and the electron path and the two circular ion-current loops are drawn paths whose speed follows Chemical Reaction Rate; the current distribution around the body is not computed.
  • Thermoelectric: two ion types with fixed drift strengths, the faster one positive (like H⁺); the hot-side label follows the slider from 28 °C to 180 °C while the cold side stays at 20 °C.
