Model assumptionsWhat this simulation simplifies
  • Each profile is a prescribed bell-shaped curve, and no potential field is solved: a minimum of about −350 mV centred on the ore body; a peak of up to +200 mV at the dam toe with a smaller negative dip at the reservoir face, both proportional to Internal Leak Severity; and a peak of up to about +150 mV over the fault, proportional to Geothermal Heat Intensity.
  • Curve widths are fixed numbers of screen pixels. They are not computed from the depth or size of the drawn sources, so the same anomaly looks wider on a narrow screen.
  • The ore-body curve is symmetric and centred on the midpoint of the drawn body; its tilt does not enter the calculation, although a dipping source gives an asymmetric profile in the field.
  • Noise is a fixed ripple of up to 5 mV on the ore and geothermal profiles and none on the dam. Readings are relative to a fixed 0 mV level, with no reference-electrode drift, telluric, or cultural noise.
