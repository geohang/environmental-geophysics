Model assumptionsWhat this simulation simplifies
  • Theoretical gravity comes from the GRS67 formula at each station's latitude, multiplied by 100,000 to give mGal.
  • The free-air correction uses a constant gradient of 0.3086 mGal/m, and the Bouguer correction treats the rock below each station as an infinite flat slab of density 2.67 g/cm³.
  • No terrain, tide, or drift correction is applied, and no regional trend is removed before interpretation.
  • Four isolated stations stand in for a profile, and each geology choice rests only on the sign of that station's Bouguer anomaly; no body shape or depth is modelled.
